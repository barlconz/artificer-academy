Exam week

Session routine (about 40 minutes)

  • 0 to 5 min: warm-up, recall three things from last time. Praise first, note one gap.
  • 5 to 25 min: learn or revise today's topic using the academy module or class slides.
  • 25 to 35 min: practice questions, mark together.
  • 35 to 40 min: exit ticket, three short questions. Stop on time.

If a session is missed

  • Do not double up. Move the missed session to the next free day and drop the mixed quiz first.
  • Must-do sessions: Body Systems A, Physics A, Physics B, Health A, Health C, and the week 5 mixed quiz.

Open items

  • Confirm the exam dates and whether Science and Health fall on the same day.
  • Confirm all five Science blocks are examinable (check the Test Planner in Classroom).
  • Check Classroom for a Body Systems checklist, and whether a written Health exam is planned.
  • Academy modules for Physics, Body Systems and Health are built. Use them alongside the class slides and practice tests, and check them against what the teacher taught.
  • Week 4 is all Health and week 5 session 2 is full. Drop the Genetics revision first if time runs short, as Genetics is already well covered.

Extra practice in the academy

  • Science, Mixed quizzes: Physics and Body Systems (24 questions) and Full Science (40 questions across all five blocks).
  • Health, Scenario practice: nine written scenarios across the exam sections (Lifestyle, Risks, Problem solving), each with a planning frame, model answer, Achieved / Merit / Excellence sentence starters and a self-check, plus 24 scenario questions. Consent and Contraception will be added once the teacher posts the slides. Do one written scenario a day in weeks 4 and 5 if there is time.
